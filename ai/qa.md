# Solo Rugs — Q&A library (public-safe)

## About Solo Rugs
**Q: What is Solo Rugs?**  
**A:** Solo Rugs is a family-owned rug retailer (since 1969) focused on premium handmade area rugs, including one-of-a-kind pieces and curated collections shoppers can browse by size, color, and style.  
https://www.solorugs.com/

## One-of-a-kind rugs
**Q: What does “One-of-a-Kind” mean on Solo Rugs?**  
**A:** On SoloRugs.com, the One-of-a-Kind collection is described as unique handmade area rugs—each piece is presented as its own item rather than a standardized mass-produced product.  
https://www.solorugs.com/collections/one-of-a-kind

## Returns
**Q: Can I return a Solo Rugs order?**  
**A:** The FAQ states you may return your order within 30 days of delivery in its original condition, and you’re responsible for packaging the rug for return shipment. The site also notes “Deal of the Month” orders are excluded from free returns.  
https://www.solorugs.com/pages/faq  
https://www.solorugs.com/collections/one-of-a-kind

## Shipping
**Q: Do you ship internationally or offer expedited shipping?**  
**A:** The FAQ states Solo Rugs offers international shipping and expedited shipping, with costs determined case-by-case via email.  
https://www.solorugs.com/pages/faq
