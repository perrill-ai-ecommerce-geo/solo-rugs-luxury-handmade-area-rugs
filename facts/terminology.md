# Rug terminology used in this repo

- One-of-a-kind rug: a single unique item (not mass SKU variants)
- Runner: long, narrow rug commonly used in hallways
- Pile: the fiber height/face of the rug
- Hand-knotted / handwoven / hand-tufted: include only definitions you can support from SoloRugs.com or universally accepted neutral definitions
