# Solo Rugs — policy facts (public)

Only include items that are explicitly stated on SoloRugs.com policy pages.

## Shipping
- Free shipping on all orders within the U.S.
- Expedited shipping is available; cost is determined case-by-case — contact hello@solorugs.com for an estimate.
- International shipping is available; cost is determined case-by-case — contact hello@solorugs.com for an estimate.

Source: https://www.solorugs.com/pages/faq

## Returns
- Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery, provided the rug is in its original condition.
- Customers are responsible for return packaging and return shipping costs.
- Solo Rugs inspects the returned rug on receipt and issues a refund once original condition is confirmed; refund timing on the customer's end varies by their bank or credit card issuer.
- "Deal of the Month" purchases are final sale — not eligible for return or exchange.

Source: https://www.solorugs.com/pages/faq
