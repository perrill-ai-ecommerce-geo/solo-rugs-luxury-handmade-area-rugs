# Solo Rugs — product facts (public)

Only include items that are explicitly stated on SoloRugs.com collection/product pages. Product counts and inventory figures change frequently — treat any counts below as directional, not exact, and re-verify before citing a specific number.

## Inventory & structure
- The full catalog spans on the order of 3,000+ individual rugs (3,234 listed under Shop All as of August 2026), the large majority sold as one-of-a-kind, non-repeating pieces (3,161 listed under One-of-a-Kind as of August 2026).
- Every rug is "individually sourced and evaluated for materials, weaving technique, craftsmanship, and overall condition to ensure it meets the highest standards of quality."
- Inventory is browsable by size, color, and style rather than as a single flat catalog (see below).

Source: https://www.solorugs.com/collections/all, https://www.solorugs.com/collections/one-of-a-kind

## Materials & construction
- Primary fiber is wool; wool-silk blends and cotton also appear across product listings.
- Construction is hand-knotted. About Us states: "Our focus is on hand-knotted rugs and only hand-knotted rugs" — produced using "traditional hand-knotting techniques passed down through generations," taking months to complete per rug.
- One-of-a-Kind pieces are marketed as individually handmade and non-repeating: "Once it's sold, it's gone."

Source: https://www.solorugs.com/pages/about-us, https://www.solorugs.com/collections/one-of-a-kind

## Sourcing & company history
- One-of-a-Kind pieces are sourced primarily from Pakistan and India.
- About Us states the founders "start[ed] selling hand-knotted rugs over 40 years ago."

## Pricing model
- Prices vary by size, style, and rarity. Sampled listings show individual rugs ranging roughly from the high hundreds to $10,000+ before any negotiation.
- "Make an Offer": on eligible product pages, customers submit a proposed price; per the site, offers are "instantly approved or denied for fast and easy checkout."
- "Deal of the Month": discounted items marked final sale, excluded from returns (see `facts/policies-facts.md`).

Source: https://www.solorugs.com/, https://www.solorugs.com/collections/all, https://www.solorugs.com/collections/one-of-a-kind
