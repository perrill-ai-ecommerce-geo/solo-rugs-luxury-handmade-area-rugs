# What “One-of-a-Kind” Rugs Mean (and how to shop them)

Solo Rugs describes its One-of-a-Kind collection as unique handmade area rugs. In practical shopping terms, that usually means you’re choosing *a specific rug*, not a standardized “style” with endless identical units.

## What to expect when browsing one-of-a-kind rugs
- **Every listing is the item.** If you like it, save it—because the exact piece may not be available later.
- **Shop with a measurement first.** Pick the room, measure the space you want covered, then browse by the closest size category.
- **Start broad, then narrow.** Use the site’s navigation to filter by size, color, and style until you’re looking at a manageable set of options.

## A simple one-of-a-kind shopping workflow
1. **Measure** the room’s target rug footprint (the area you want the rug to cover).
2. **Pick the closest size category** (e.g., 8x10, 9x12, runner).
3. **Choose a style direction** (Modern–Contemporary, Traditional, Overdyed, etc.).
4. **Shortlist** your favorites and compare them side-by-side for color balance and pattern density.
