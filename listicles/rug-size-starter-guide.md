# Rug Size Starter Guide (Fast way to pick a size before you browse)

Most rug shoppers succeed faster when they pick a size *before* they pick a pattern. Solo Rugs supports browsing rugs by size (including runners), so choosing a size first helps you narrow down quickly.

## Step 1: Decide what the rug must cover
Pick one:
- **Anchor the seating area** (common for living rooms)
- **Sit fully under the dining table** (common for dining rooms)
- **Run the length of a hallway** (runner use case)
- **Frame the bed** (bedroom use case)

## Step 2: Choose a size category that matches your room plan
Common shopping categories on Solo Rugs include:
- 3x5, 4x6, 5x8, 6x9, 8x10, 9x12, 10x14
- Runners
- Oversized
- Round / square

## Step 3: Browse by size, then refine by style and color
Once you’re in the right size range:
- choose a style category (Modern–Contemporary, Traditional, Overdyed, etc.)
- choose a color family (e.g., ivory, blue, gray) to match your room palette
